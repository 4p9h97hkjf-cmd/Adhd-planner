The "I've Been Gone" Reset

You disappeared for a few days (or weeks). That's okay. This page exists exactly for this moment. No guilt, no shame — just a soft landing back.

⏳ How Long Were You Gone?
Pick one — totally judgment-free:
A day or two
About a week
A few weeks
Honestly… months
I've never really started
→ Whatever you picked: you're back now. That's the only thing that matters.
"Missing days doesn't mean you failed the planner. It means you're human. The planner doesn't care how long you were gone — it's just happy you're here."
🪜 The 5-Step Comeback Protocol
💨
Step 1: Take one breath and release the guilt.

Guilt uses up working memory — and you need that RAM for today. Let it go right now. Actually let it go.

🗑️
Step 2: Don't catch up. Just restart.

You don't need to fill in what you missed. Cross out the old stuff. Today is a fresh document.

🎯
Step 3: Name ONE thing for today.

Just one. Not three. Not a list. One single task that would make coming back feel worth it.

⏱️
Step 4: Do 10 minutes — just 10.

Set a timer. If you stop after 10, you still came back. If you keep going, bonus. Either way: win.

🌱
Step 5: Tell yourself the truth.

You didn't "fall off." You paused. Pausing and returning is literally what long-term progress looks like.
